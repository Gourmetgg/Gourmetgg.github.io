a small place on the internet

why a personal website still feels like a good idea

12 October 20242 min read[ writing, technology ]

A personal website is an unusually quiet kind of internet space. It does not have to recommend the next thing. It does not have to turn a thought into a performance. It can simply contain some pages.

I like that possibility.

an address for your thoughts

A post can have a stable address. You can send it to someone, return to it, or leave it alone for a year. The archive can grow slowly. An old essay can sit next to a new one without either needing to win attention.

There are practical things to take care of: readable text, working links, a layout that fits a phone. Beyond that, much of the work is deciding what you want to put there.

begin before it is complete

The perfect design is an easy excuse to delay the first page. So is the perfect name. A website can begin as a few notes and change as the notes accumulate.

I prefer sites that feel inhabited to sites that feel finished. A correction, an unexpected topic, a short post after a long silence: these are signs that a person has been here.

A small place is still a place. You can start with one page.
