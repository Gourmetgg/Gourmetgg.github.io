taking the long way home

a few thoughts from a walk with no podcast

24 July 20252 min read[ life ]

The shortest route home is efficient and familiar. I know which crossing takes too long, which shop has its lights on late, and roughly how many minutes remain.

The longer route has fewer facts attached to it. It passes a tree I do not know the name of. There is a small stretch where the traffic becomes quiet. Sometimes someone is watering the plants outside a doorway.

leave a little space

I often fill a walk with something to listen to. There is nothing wrong with that. But without headphones, the walk has a different texture. A thought can take its time. It does not have to compete with another voice.

A problem from earlier in the day sometimes rearranges itself. More often, nothing remarkable happens. I arrive home having noticed the weather.

That feels like enough. Not every interval needs to carry information. Some can simply connect one part of the day to another.

the extra ten minutes

The longer way is not always available. There are evenings when I am tired, late, or carrying too much. But when it is available, I try to remember that speed is only one way to measure a route.

There are other things to get home with.
