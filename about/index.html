a little about this place

margin is a small notebook for technology, ordinary days, and the things in between.

Some notes begin with a piece of code. Others begin with a walk, a book, or a question that refuses to go away. The common thread is curiosity, and a preference for thinking things through on paper.

what you will find here

Explanations of small technical ideas. Essays about attention and learning. Occasional reminders to go outside. Nothing needs to fit neatly into one category.
